Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Your Google account name and email address, used to create and secure your account.
  • Your board, branch, semester and medium, used to show the right notes.
  • A random device ID, used to keep one account on one device.
  • Payment screenshots you upload, used only to confirm your payment.
  • Study timer, streak, music list and saved offline notes stay on your phone and are not sent to us.

How we use it

We use your information to sign you in, unlock the notes you bought, confirm payments, prevent sharing of paid content and answer your questions. We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.

Who processes it

We use trusted services to run AR Academy: Google Firebase (sign in), Neon (database), Vercel (hosting), Cloudinary (file storage) and Anthropic (automatic reading of payment screenshots). They process data only to provide their service to us.

App lock permission

If you turn on the optional Focus Lock, Android’s Accessibility permission is used only to notice when an app you chose to block is opened and send you back to AR Academy. We do not read your screen, messages or passwords, and nothing about the apps you use is sent to us.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by writing to us. Signing out removes saved notes from your phone.

Children

Students under 18 should use AR Academy with a parent or guardian’s permission.
