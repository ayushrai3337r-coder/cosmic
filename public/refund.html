Refund policy

Digital notes

Notes are digital content that unlock as soon as your payment is confirmed, so payments are not refundable once the notes are opened.

When we will help

  • You paid twice for the same notes.

Write to us within 7 days with your payment screenshot and the email you signed in with. If we agree, the refund is sent to the UPI account you paid from.
